Privacy Policy – Winning Edge Stats

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who uses this app

Winning Edge Stats is used only by the owner of the Winning Edge channels, for his own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is not available to other users.

What data it reads

With the owner's permission, the app reads aggregate channel and content statistics such as views, watch time, followers, subscribers, reach and engagement, using the official YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and Meta (Instagram/Facebook) Graph API with read-only permissions.

What it does not do

The app never posts, edits, comments on, or deletes any content, and does not access private messages.

How data is used and stored

Statistics are used only to prepare private growth reports for the channel owner. Data is stored privately, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Meta's Business Integrations settings. On revocation, stored data can be deleted on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Winning Edge channel pages.
